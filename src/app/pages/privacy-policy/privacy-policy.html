Cookie Policy

Last updated: 05 October 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how Atman Cloud Consultants Pvt. Ltd. (“we”, “us”, “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on AtmanConsultants.com. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.


1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners. Related technologies include local storage, pixels, and SDKs; we refer to all of these as “cookies” in this notice.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Strictly Necessary: Required to deliver core site functionality and security (e.g., page navigation, session management).
  • Performance & Analytics: To understand how visitors use our site and improve content and user experience (e.g., page views, error rates).
  • Functional: To remember user preferences (e.g., language, cookie consent choices).
  • Advertising (if enabled): To deliver or measure ads and limit ad frequency. We do not use these unless explicitly enabled.

3. Legal basis

We place strictly necessary cookies based on our legitimate interests in operating a secure, functional website. For all other cookies, we request your consent via the cookie banner. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time (see “Manage your preferences” below).

5) Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by third parties (for example, analytics and embedded content providers). These providers may process data outside India. Where required, we use contractual safeguards and rely on your consent for such cookies.

6) Manage your preferences / withdraw consent

You can manage non-essential cookies at any time by adjusting your choices in the cookie banner or via your browser settings. If you previously accepted and wish to change your choice, clear your cookies for AtmanConsultants.com and reload the page to see the banner again.

  • Browser controls: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. See your browser’s help pages.
  • Do Not Track: Our site currently does not respond to DNT signals.

7) Data retention

Cookie data are retained for the durations shown above or as otherwise required for security and site operation. Analytics data may be retained longer in aggregate form.

8) Children

Our website is not directed to children. We do not knowingly use cookies to profile children.

9) Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.
